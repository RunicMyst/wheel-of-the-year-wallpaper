# Credits

## The wheel

The Wheel of the Year artwork — the rings, the Sabbat and zodiac layout, the hand-traced
Vegvísir and the ring of 24 Elder Futhark runes — was drawn for this project by RunicMyst,
in FreeCAD and then Inkscape. Shared under the MIT licence with the code.

The Vegvísir itself comes from the 1860 Huld Manuscript. Despite the "Viking compass" nickname
it is 19th-century Icelandic folk magic, not Viking-age — but it is genuine to modern Norse and
heathen practice, which is why it sits at the centre.

## Astronomy

Solstice, equinox, moon phase and rise/set calculations follow Jean Meeus, *Astronomical
Algorithms* (2nd edition, Willmann-Bell). The formulas are implemented from the book; no code
was copied.

Time zone positions come from the [tz database](https://www.iana.org/time-zones) (public
domain) and Windows time-zone names from [CLDR](https://cldr.unicode.org/) (Unicode licence).
